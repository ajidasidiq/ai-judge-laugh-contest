An entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) competition
**Make the AI Judge Laugh** (`u-alfasurya91-make-the-ai-judge-laugh-6ed40225`).

## What it does

`main.py` writes one original one-line joke about office life to
`/output/entry.txt`. No network, no input, no randomness: the same line on
every run.

## The joke

> I blocked my calendar for focus time, so my boss booked a meeting called 'Focus Time Sync'.

## Why it should win

- **True to the office.** Blocking focus time on the calendar is something
  every office worker has tried — and watched fail.
- **Surprising turn.** The rescue of your focus time arrives as the very
  thing that destroys it: a meeting named after the thing it cancels.
- **Tight.** One breath, 91 characters, and the punchline is the last two
  words.
- **Original.** A meeting called "Focus Time Sync" is not a line anyone
  has heard before — it is the kind of thing only a real calendar would
  invent.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
